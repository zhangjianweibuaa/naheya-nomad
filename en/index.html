The Founder · 关于主理人

Why a philosophy MA spent
20 years in travel

I'm Jianwei Zhang, holder of an MA in Philosophy from Inner Mongolia Normal University, with a research direction in Mongolian religious culture. I started as a front-line guide in 2006 and grew into running my own inbound agency — twenty years walking this land.

Philosophy trained me to ask what things essentially are: travel is not a collection of check-ins, but an encounter between a person and a place. Ground-handling isn't just showing the way — it's translating the language of the steppe for people arriving from cities. Why does a yurt door face southeast? Why is milk tea salty? Why do herders migrate on that particular day? The "whys" are where Inner Mongolia gets truly beautiful. That long accumulation of Mongolian religious culture also feeds the content design of our family study travel: the commentary depth of history-and-museum themes comes from here.

So I split the work into two lines: Naheya for kids, turning grassland into a nature classroom; Nomad for adults, returning the itinerary to your own rhythm. Both share one baseline: pure travel, transparency, and my personal accountability.

When to Come · 四季内蒙古

Every season has its reason

Inner Mongolia is not only summer. The founder runs different plays each season — off-peak is quieter and better value.

JUN–SEP

Summer · Peak green

July–August: grass at its fullest, Naadam festivals everywhere. Home of the study camp and classic loop — book 30 days ahead.

LATE SEP–OCT

Autumn · Birch gold

Hulunbuir's birch forests turn gold for barely twenty days a year — the photographer's window, few crowds, perfect light.

DEC–FEB

Winter · Snow Naadam

Season of the independence camp and CNY family camp; tourism vouchers apply, heated yurts and hot springs included.

APR–MAY

Spring · Azaleas & birds

Azaleas bloom, migratory birds return, the steppe wakes up. Off-peak pricing, gentle pace.
